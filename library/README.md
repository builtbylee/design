# Library

A curated collection of excellent, highly polished UI, for browsing and for pointing at when building something new. Each entry is excellent at one thing: its motion, its interaction, its density or its type.

- **`entries/<id>.html`**: a standalone, working demo. Open it in a browser.
- **`entries/<id>.md`**: why it works, what to take, its anatomy and behaviour (`templates/ENTRY.template.md`).
- **`library.json`**: the index. Each entry has a `kind` (`component` or `screen`), `tags`, and an `origin`: `original`, or `study` with the maker and a link.
- **`gallery.html`**: generated by `node tools/build-gallery.mjs`. Every entry live, filterable by kind and tag. Don't edit it by hand.

**Studies** are credited rebuilds of public designs in open code, kept for reference. They never include the original's logo, screenshots or proprietary fonts, and they're never shipped as-is (Principle 9).
